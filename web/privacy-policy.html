Privacy policy

Demo data

Use fictional information only. The demo stores your case in this browser, including any text you enter and the sample report you choose. Demo roles can be switched within the same browser.

Erase your case

Open the Administrator workspace and select Reset demo, or clear this site’s storage in your browser. When browser storage is unavailable, changes are kept in the current tab.

Tracking and hosting

The site does not set tracking cookies or load Google Analytics. Cloudflare processes requests needed to deliver the site. Your installed dictation software may process speech under its own privacy policy.
